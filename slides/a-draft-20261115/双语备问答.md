# A 版双语备问答

用于两分钟Q&A，按实际问题选答，不要求把全部问题背完。先用20–30秒回答，再按追问展开。

## 1. Why use the simpler presentation route?（6号）

We chose the route that all members can explain from their actual work. It includes data auditing, feature evidence, a simple predictive baseline, group diagnostics and an experiment proposal. The choice reflects presentation readiness. It does not prove that one route is universally more reliable than the other.

中文：强调全员能讲清已有工作。A/B可靠性不能仅凭模型复杂度或50/49判断。

## 2. What does class weighting do?（3号，第4/9页）

Repurchasers are rare, so class weighting gives their errors more influence during fitting. We keep the same data and eight input fields. It changes the fitted ranking, but its raw output should not be interpreted as a calibrated natural purchase probability.

中文：不是删掉负例，也不是只给正例打分；分数用于排序，不能直接解释成真实复购概率。

## 3. Is 50 versus 46 a significant improvement?（3号，第4页）

It is four additional observed buyers in equally sized January lists. We report that difference descriptively. We do not claim that the existing evidence proves statistical significance or stable superiority on future data. The old uncertainty figure is not used.

中文：不能沿用5号原错误区间，也不能把其他协议的区间迁过来。没有新跑区间或模型。

## 4. What do precision and recall mean?（3/5号，第4页）

Precision is the share of selected customers who repurchase: 50 divided by 2,937, or 1.70 percent. Recall is the share of all observed repurchasers found by the list: 50 divided by 230, or 21.74 percent. They use different denominators.

中文：precision分母是名单人数，recall分母是全部真实复购者。不要将两者叫成模型整体准确率。

## 5. Why choose ten percent?（6号，第11页）

Ten percent is a consistent capacity for comparing lists, not a demonstrated operating optimum. All eligible customers receive a score. We also retain existing five- and twenty-percent results. Actual capacity should depend on contact costs, reachability and measured response.

中文：5%未加权/加权均31；10%46/50；20%73/75。只是在已存结果中改变名单容量，不是重新训练。

## 6. Did the extra fields fail?（3号主答、2号补充，第3/5页）

The tested delivery and review variants did not beat the weighted baseline at the ten-percent January capacity. The latest proposed feature groups have different definitions and were not tested by those models. We cannot treat those results as evidence against every delivery or review feature.

中文：配送实际F1=已知送达数/平均配送时长/迟到比例；2号G1=待送达数/log配送时长/缺失指示。评价组也不同，不能只按组名判断相同。

## 7. Did clustering help?（4/5号，第6/10页）

The existing H05 groups use K-means with three clusters on standardized recency, log-one-plus frequency and spending. We fit the transformation and centers on October data and apply them to January. Group names describe profiles rather than manual thresholds. It helped us describe customers, locate missed buyers and develop a content hypothesis. Adding a group label did not improve the main January count, and separate group models found 49 rather than 50. Useful customer insight does not require every clustering method to improve prediction.

中文：分群保留诊断价值；不能说三群天然最优，也不能说全体群模型流程都折内隔离。

## 8. What is the difference between 73 and 79?（5号，第6页）

Seventy-three is the number of long-inactive customers inside the current weighted global list. Seventy-nine is the capacity of a separate list ranked only within that group. The global slice finds two buyers. The fixed group-only comparison finds one for unweighted and two for weighted logistic regression.

中文：当前全局73、旧H04全局79、另固定群内79不能混用；全局同容量不代表各群同容量。

## 9. Why not choose the group model with four long-inactive hits?（4/5号，第10页）

That model gives the group 518 global-list places, compared with 73 for the baseline. At the same fixed group capacity of 79, it finds one buyer while the baseline finds two. The four global hits reflect a different allocation of capacity, not proof of better ranking within the group.

中文：是覆盖取舍，不是群内算法改善的唯一归因证据。

## 10. What does the five-fold result establish?（3号主答，第5/11页）

It is an internal October diagnostic. Each validation fold has 4,232 customers and selects 424. The unweighted and weighted baselines average 7.0 and 6.8 hits. The reported spread is sample standard deviation, not a confidence interval. These are not five independent future months.

中文：原群模型的H05表示用全十月无标签特征拟合，再做分类CV，因此应注明条件，不称整个聚类加分类完全折内。

## 11. Is January an unseen final test?（1/5号）

No. January has already been used during development. We preserve its status as a seen development window. April had also been examined previously. The current presentation does not claim a fresh future blind test and does not add another April evaluation.

中文：换路线、重排PPT或改“冻结”字眼都不能恢复盲测。参数的实际采用与事前预注册是两回事。

## 12. How did you control future information?（1/3号，第2页）

Predictors use events before the snapshot, and the label uses eligible approved positive-value orders in the following ninety days. October labels mature before January evaluation. Preprocessing is learned on fitting data. The time audit also records assumptions about data arrival and historical field availability, rather than claiming perfect source-system completeness.

中文：F1迟到比例对预计送达日期的历史可得性有假设；这不是已经证明有目标泄漏，也不影响未使用该附加字段的F0主参照。

## 13. Does the content test prove marketing uplift?（4/6号，第7页）

The test has not run. Its proposal compares a themed guide with a generic guide, with both groups contacted. It would estimate a difference between themes, not the total effect of contact versus no contact. Profit and incremental marketing value have not been established.

中文：两组同渠道、同版式、同时间；不加入未开展的第三组，也不把历史名单命中当营销增量。

## 14. Can you run a sufficiently powered trial?（4号，第7页）

Reachability is unknown. The historical group contains 12,330 customers. Under the existing normal-approximation design, a fifty-percent relative improvement requires 21,308 people in total. Small improvements may therefore be difficult to detect. We need eligibility and power checks before launch.

中文：双侧5%、功效80%、1:1；+50%是相对提升，约+0.365个百分点。设计尚未执行，不承诺试验可行或有效。

## 15. Why select weighted LR when CatBoost has a higher ROC-AUC?（3号，第4页）

Our operating comparison fixes the list at 2,937 customers. Weighted LR finds the most repurchasers, 50, among the seven methods in the supplied screening table. It also has the highest AP. CatBoost has the highest ROC-AUC, which evaluates ranking over all thresholds. Our selection is specific to the fixed-capacity objective, not a claim of winning every metric or future statistical superiority.

中文：第4页选择依据是固定Top10%名单命中；相同N、正例数、K时precision/recall的排序也与命中相同。不能笼统说weighted LR所有指标最优。

## 16. Are the screening table and later R3 results the same run?（3号主答、5号补充）

No. The supplied CSV is byte-identical to the saved H04 v0.2 model-screening table. The later slides use the R3 follow-up. Both weighted models find 50 January buyers, but their scores and selected lists differ. The current group analysis uses the R3 list with 73 long-inactive places. We preserve the stage distinction rather than combining their AP, AUC and group allocations.

中文：附表AP=0.029796574055586278、AUC=0.5820409326533109；R3对应约0.029804443848、0.581738026244。旧H04久单全局79席，R3全局73席，命中同为2；同50不等于同名单。另固定群内79是第三个定义。
