## 讲稿与中文提示

### 第 1 页 · Finding repeat buyers with limited capacity

负责：6 号；预算 25 秒。

Hello everyone. Our project asks how a manager can identify likely repeat buyers when customer-contact capacity is limited. We use Olist's historical orders to rank customers and understand who the list misses. The goal is a useful candidate list and a content-test proposal. Actual marketing impact remains untested.

开场用经理的任务切入：名额有限，怎样挑出更可能复购的人。沿团队已做的数据、特征、LR、分群和核数讲述。A 的输出是排序名单，不把原始加权分数说成自然概率。

### 第 2 页 · One prediction task, two analytical contributions

负责：6 号；预算 35 秒。

Our analysis has two connected paths. From the same audited customer data, we compare predictive models and build customer groups. Predictions and group labels then meet in the group-level diagnosis. This explains coverage and suggests possible features. The tested R3 variants and member two's newer proposals are separate: the newer groups are not yet model-tested. We combine observed development results with customer profiles to guide decisions. The content trial remains unimplemented.

先讲共同数据，再讲预测与分群两条线，指出它们在群内误差核验相遇。实线为已有分析依赖；虚线是特征反馈方向，新G1/G2尚未模型测试。左侧是已见一月结果，不说独立盲测。新页建议由6号衔接开场。

### 第 3 页 · Past information, future repurchase

负责：1 号；预算 60 秒。

We link order items, orders and customers, then create one row per customer at each prediction date. Inputs use the previous six calendar months. The outcome is at least one approved order with positive merchandise value in the next ninety days. Later delivery events and review responses cannot enter earlier predictors. The October training snapshot has twenty-one thousand one hundred and sixty customers and two hundred and eleven repurchasers. Its labels mature by December thirtieth. January has twenty-nine thousand three hundred and sixty-eight customers and two hundred and thirty repurchasers. The model fits all October customers, without a separate calibration subset. It scores every eligible January customer and selects the top ten percent: two thousand nine hundred and thirty-seven places. January is an already examined development window.

A 使用全十月 21,160 人拟合，没有 B 的 16,928/4,232 校准拆分。按客户×预测日构造快照，六自然月与90天分清。时点审计不能保证源系统绝无迟到入库；一月仍是已见开发期。

### 第 4 页 · Customer history suggests feature ideas

负责：2 号；预算 50 秒。

Our existing H05 segmentation uses K-means with three groups. It standardizes recency, log-transformed frequency and spending using October data, then assigns January customers to the learned centers. The labels summarize the resulting groups: recent single-order, long-inactive single-order and multi-order. They are not manual recency cut-offs or identical descriptions of every person. The long-inactive group has ninety repurchasers among twelve thousand three hundred and thirty customers. This motivates checking fulfilment and review information, with attention to availability and missingness. Member two proposed pending deliveries and delivery time, and mean review score with review coverage. Those proposals differ from the variants already tested. Their predictive benefit remains untested.

2 号讲 EDA 提供什么：三群差异帮助提出字段假设；描述相关不等于模型增益。R为距最近购买天数，F为历史订单数。三群用已有 H05 画像，不能说3群天然最优。G1/G2 新推荐没有接入本次模型，别把 F1/F2 的结果代入。 H05为K-means三群；R、log1p(F)、log1p(M)经十月标准化拟合后，一月套用训练群中心，不是按37/135天人工设阈值。

### 第 5 页 · Weighted LR leads the model-screening comparison

负责：3 号；预算 70 秒。

We compare seven methods from member three's model-screening table. Each method scores the same January cohort and selects two thousand nine hundred and thirty-seven customers. Weighted logistic regression finds fifty repeat buyers, compared with forty-six for logistic regression, forty-five for CatBoost, forty-four for random forest, and thirty for the recency rule. The weighted forest and weighted CatBoost find thirty-six and thirty-five. We select weighted logistic regression for the fixed-capacity objective because it finds the most buyers. It also has the highest average precision, although CatBoost has the highest ROC-AUC. So the choice depends on our objective, not winning every metric. This is the saved H04 screening stage. The next slide uses the later R3 follow-up, which also finds fifty buyers but has different saved scores and lists. These are examined development results, not a new blind test.

使用3号此次指定CSV，逐字节匹配H04 v0.2：Weighted LR50、LR46、CB45、RF44、Weighted RF36、Weighted CB35、Recency30。选择依据是同容量Top10%命中最多；AP也最高，但ROC-AUC不是最高。此处CB是H04原八字段候选，不是B版141字段校准系统。第6页转后续R3，不把相同50命中当相同分数/名单。

### 第 6 页 · The tested additions did not beat the baseline

负责：3 号；预算 70 秒。

We now use the later R3 results to check extensions on the same January cohort and list capacity. The weighted baseline finds fifty buyers. Adding the tested delivery fields finds forty-eight, review fields forty-six, a group label fifty, and separate group models forty-nine. The separate models use a global fallback for the small multi-order group. None beats the baseline at the ten-percent capacity, although some variants do better at other capacities. This conclusion applies only to the fields actually tested. It does not reject the latest feature combinations proposed by member two. We also retain the October five-fold diagnostic. The unweighted baseline averages seven hits per fold and the weighted baseline six point eight, so weighting does not win every check. These folds each select four hundred and twenty-four people and cannot be compared directly with the January count of fifty. Together, these checks support keeping a simple reference while reporting where improvements were not observed.

3 号按本次成员反馈主讲方法扩展与验证：只讲实际 F1/F2 和群模型。模型代码中的 B/C 是候选编号，不是展示路线A/B，画面改用完整名称避混。CV均值7 vs6.8表示内部诊断也有反例；不是五个月、不是一月50的置信区间。 本页为后续R3，和第5页早期H04筛选表是不同保存版本。

### 第 7 页 · The global list still misses most long-inactive buyers

负责：5 号；预算 55 秒。

Our weighted global list finds fifty repeat buyers, but it misses eighty-eight of the ninety repurchasers in the long-inactive group. Three diagnostics explain the concern. First, only seventy-three of this group's twelve thousand three hundred and thirty customers enter the global list. Second, the separate fixed-seventy-nine test finds two buyers with weighting, compared with one without it. More global places do not prove better ranking within the group. Third, the tested extensions find forty-eight, forty-six, fifty and forty-nine buyers globally, without exceeding the baseline. These are diagnostic findings, not a proven causal explanation for each missed buyer.

5 号核三群合计：2190+73+674=2937，21+2+27=50。久单90位实际复购者只命中2，漏88。全局73席和另固定79是不同名单。固定79未加权1、加权2；不能混成旧H06全局17席/0命中。 按5号要求补三点：全局覆盖、固定群内容量、已测扩展。73不是预先设置的群配额；48/46/50/49是全局命中。

### 第 8 页 · Customer profiles support a testable content idea

负责：4 号；预算 60 秒。

Customer groups also support a business hypothesis. The long-inactive group has median frequency one and median recency around one hundred and thirty-five days. Historical category coverage suggests a home-goods guide, but does not establish a distinctive preference or a causal effect. The proposal randomly allocates reachable, consenting customers equally between a themed guide and a generic guide. Channel, format and timing stay the same. We compare ninety-day repurchase using all randomized customers in the denominator. The trial has not run. Detecting a fifty-percent relative improvement requires twenty-one thousand three hundred and eight people, more than the historical group size. We need reachability and power checks first. Both groups receive contact, so we test the theme, not contact versus no contact.

4 号延续自己的分群、品类覆盖和试验设计。保留两组均触达、1:1、ITT分母、未开展和样本量不足。主题按历史覆盖选，不说久单独有偏好或已证有效。+50%是相对提升，不是+50个百分点。

### 第 9 页 · A simple reference with clear limits

负责：6 号；预算 35 秒。

Weighted logistic regression led the seven-method screening with fifty hits. The later R3 baseline also found fifty against forty-six for unweighted regression. The tested additions did not improve that ten-percent result. Groups exposed missed buyers and supported a content-test proposal. We keep three limits clear: January is already examined, scores support ranking, and marketing impact is untested. Next, check reachability and power before a trial, and evaluate on genuinely future data. Thank you.

结论只收三件事：当前同容量多4人；实际测试扩展未提高10%结果；分群显示覆盖问题并支持试验假设。保留开发期/非自然概率/非营销增量限制，不以团队更熟悉A推导算法更可靠。

### 第 10 页 · Eight inputs and one ranking protocol

负责：1 / 3 号；备查，不占主讲时间。

The main reference is current R3 F0 weighted logistic regression, fitted on all October customers. The eight fields are recency, order frequency, merchandise spending, average item rows, freight share, recent thirty-day orders, recent thirty-day spending, and state. Numeric preprocessing uses log-one-plus, median imputation with missing indicators, and standard scaling. State uses most-frequent imputation and one-hot encoding. The recorded model specifies C equal to one, balanced class weights, a maximum of two thousand iterations and random state 2026. The final list sorts scores in descending order, with customer ID as a deterministic tie-break. We do not describe this raw score as a natural probability or the observed development score as a blind-test estimate.

八字段是客户摘要，不是原始九表只有八列。模型配置来自当前R3源码，当前采用是展示工作基线，不伪造事前冻结；历史包的完整环境/终止诊断缺口仍按原记录保留。

### 第 11 页 · More group places need not mean better group ranking

负责：4 / 5 号；备查，不占主讲时间。

The global and group-only comparisons have different denominators. The baseline gives the long-inactive group 73 global places and finds two buyers. Adding group ID gives it 181 places and still finds two. The separate group models give it 518 places and find four. But when each method gets exactly 79 places within that group, the results are two, two and one. Therefore the four global hits from group models do not prove better ranking within the group. Global capacity is fixed, but allocation across groups changes.

用此表回答群模型为什么没采用：518席4人是覆盖变化；固定79仅1人。禁止用“全局同名额”推导“群内同名额”。

### 第 12 页 · Capacity and internal validation show trade-offs

负责：3 / 5 / 6 号；备查，不占主讲时间。

At five percent, the unweighted and weighted baseline both find 31 buyers. At ten percent, they find 46 and 50. At twenty percent, they find 73 and 75. Other candidates can perform better at different capacities, so ten percent is a comparison choice rather than a proven optimum. The October five-fold means are seven and six point eight hits for unweighted and weighted logistic regression. The corresponding sample standard deviations are about two point five five and one point nine two. They describe variability across the five folds and are not confidence intervals. Group-method CV additionally conditions on a clustering representation learned from all October unlabeled features, so it is not a fully fold-contained clustering-and-classification pipeline.

capacity变化有取舍：5%31/31，10%46/50，20%73/75。CV每折N4232/K424；std是样本SD，不是95%CI、不是真正未来独立测试。

### 第 14 页 · Tested changes and open feature ideas

负责：2 / 3 / 5 号；备查，不占主讲时间。

This page explains why the feedback loop must be qualified. The R3 delivery variant uses known delivery count, mean delivery time and late-delivery share. Member two's newer G1 instead uses pending deliveries, logged delivery time and a missing indicator. The R3 review variant and proposed G2 also differ. The earlier R3 comparison therefore cannot prove that the new proposals are ineffective. A matched controlled comparison would be required before adoption, and those new groups have not yet been model-tested. Our January evidence remains development evidence, not a clean future holdout.

左栏是3号R3实际测试输入；右栏是2号v1.1建议的不同字段。不要用F1/F2没有提高命中来宣布G1/G2无效。下方虚线表示尚未完成的“新提案→同口径验证→证据后决定”。不是安排本轮重训。
