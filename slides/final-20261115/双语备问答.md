# 双语备问答

用于 2 分钟 Q&A。先用 20–30 秒英文短答回答问题，再按追问展开。编号是原岗位，不表示成员已经排练。

## 1. Why did you choose the deeper version if the old model found 50 buyers and this one found 49?

主答 3 号，6 号接答；第 9 页。

**English:** The fitting protocols differ. The old model uses all October customers for fitting. The calibrated pipeline reserves twenty percent for probability calibration. Under the same fit, calibration and evaluation split, weighted logistic regression finds 43 buyers and CatBoost finds 49. We chose the deeper analysis for its probability checks and business trade-offs, not because 49 beats 50.

**中文：** 不同训练资源不能直接排行。B 的价值是分析与验证更完整，不是宣称当前目标已经全面超过 A。

## 2. Does the improvement come from CatBoost or the extra features?

主答 3 号，2 号接答；第 3、4 页。

**English:** The headline comparison changes both the learner and the information set, from eight to 141 fields. It measures the full pipeline difference. We also have saved same-information and same-model comparisons, which show that extra information does not improve every method or metric. We cannot attribute all six extra hits to CatBoost alone.

**中文：** 同信息集比较模型、同模型比较信息，才有助于归因。不能把联合变更的 +6 全归给算法。

## 3. Why calibrate the probabilities?

主答 3 号；第 4、9 页。

**English:** Ranking scores do not automatically represent purchase probabilities, especially after class weighting. Calibration adjusts the probability scale using a separate labelled subset. We calibrate each seed and then average its probabilities. It does not remove time drift: January probabilities sum to about 293 buyers, compared with 230 observed.

**中文：** 校准是调整概率刻度，不是保证准确，也不是拿评价集标签纠正结果。正斜率 Platt 保留单模型的排序方向；三模型平均后要按真实融合结果评价。

## 4. What exactly does ten percent mean?

主答 6 号，5 号补充；第 11 页。

**English:** Every eligible customer receives a score. Ten percent is the size of the output list, rounded up to 2,937 people in January. It is a consistent comparison setting. A real operating capacity needs contact costs and response evidence. Our existing one-to-one-hundred-percent results show the trade-off between precision and reach.

**中文：** 不是只预测 10% 的人。Precision=命中/名单人数；Recall=命中/全部真实复购者。10% 不是已经证明最优的预算。

## 5. Are the three months independent test sets?

主答 5 号，1 号接答；第 5 页。

**English:** No. These are already examined historical checks, and customers, histories and target windows overlap. We compare methods within each date. January contributed to development, and April had also been seen previously. We do not claim a clean future blind test. That remains a next evaluation step.

**中文：** 不同月份不自动等于独立样本；时间正确也不自动恢复“未见”。本次没有新开四月评价。

## 6. How did you avoid using future information?

主答 1 号，3 号接答；第 2 页。

**English:** Predictors use the six calendar months before each prediction date. Labels use eligible approved positive-value orders in the next ninety days. We audit field availability, fit preprocessing on fitting data, and use only calibration labels for calibration. For January, October labels mature by December thirtieth. Source-system completeness and historical field availability still require assumptions.

**中文：** 强调历史字段可用性、成熟标签、fit-only 预处理。已知预计送达承诺和静态品类的历史版本存在假设，不把审计写成所有历史源数据完美。

## 7. Did clustering improve prediction?

主答 4 号，5 号接答；第 3、7 页。

**English:** Clustering helped locate missed buyers, propose features and interpret coverage. It did not automatically improve prediction. In the existing team protocol, adding a group label found 50 buyers and separate group models found 49, compared with 50 for the weighted baseline. Those results do not establish three groups as a natural optimum.

**中文：** 分群的诊断价值可以成立，不需要硬说它提高了准确率。k=3 是已有具体画像方案，非天然最优群数。

## 8. Why do you report both 73 and 79 for the long-inactive group?

主答 5 号，4 号接答；第 7 页。

**English:** Seventy-three is the number of long-inactive customers inside the global CatBoost list. Two of them repurchased. Seventy-nine is a separate diagnostic where we rank only customers in that group and select exactly 79. It also finds two buyers. Global coverage and ranking within the group answer different questions.

**中文：** 73 是全局名单切片；79 是另起的群内固定容量，不是同一个分母。当前 R3 A 与 B 恰好都出现 73/2，也不能混淆实验出处。

## 9. Why not choose the highest-scoring neural model?

主答 5 号，3 号接答；第 5、10 页。

**English:** Higher January scores did not establish stable superiority. In an earlier October check, the autoencoder found 31 buyers while the logistic reference found 36. Some differences have conditional intervals that include zero. We therefore retain negative evidence instead of choosing only the largest observed score. GAT was not actually run and is not a claimed result.

**中文：** AE 一月 51、删行为块 58 都是已见开发结果，不能单点挑赢家。条件区间也不是完全覆盖训练与选模不确定性的最终保证。

## 10. Is p × F × M an expected revenue forecast?

主答 6 号，2 号补充；第 6 页。

**English:** It is a preference score based on probability and historical value. F is historical order count and M is total historical merchandise value, which already reflects frequency. It is not directly a currency forecast. The separate predicted-amount rule estimates ninety-day merchandise amount conditional on buying, then multiplies by purchase probability.

**中文：** pFM 对高频客户有额外偏好，不能直接叫未来金额。预计金额规则更直接对应金额目标，但当前一月这格反而较差，不能只选其他有利格。

## 11. Have you demonstrated marketing uplift or profit?

主答 6 号，4 号接答；第 6、7 页。

**English:** No. The amounts are merchandise value observed in historical lists. They exclude a causal comparison of contacted and uncontacted customers, and they are not profit. The existing content trial compares a themed guide with a generic guide, so it would estimate the theme difference among contacted customers. It has not been implemented.

**中文：** 随机内容差异试验也不能自动回答“发邮件是否比不发有效”。两组都触达，若未来要测联系本身的效果需要另行设计；本次不扩试验。

## 12. Is the proposed content trial feasible?

主答 4 号，6 号接答；第 7 页。

**English:** Feasibility is uncertain. The historical group has 12,330 customers, but the reachable consenting population is unknown. Using its historical repurchase rate, a fifty-percent relative increase would require about 21,308 customers under the current normal-approximation design. We need a reachability and power check before launch, and we cannot promise detection of small effects.

**中文：** 原设计双侧 α=0.05、功效 0.80、1:1。+50% 是相对提升，不是增加 50 个百分点；对应约 +0.365 个百分点。低事件率还应在真正上线前做更精确的功效核查。本次只复用既有计算，不新运行。

## 13. What did each member contribute?

各人先答本人，6 号统筹；第 12 页。

**English:** We retained the original responsibilities: data and sample construction, exploratory analysis and feature evidence, prediction, segmentation, evaluation and feedback, and business coordination. The deeper experiments have separate execution records. Presenting a result does not mean every member personally ran it. Each member is responsible for understanding and checking the evidence they present.

**中文：** 只陈述已交材料与实际执行记录；不要把本地深化研究冒写成六人全部亲自做过。你本次完成初稿，然后按原责任链分发核阅。

## 14. Which metric should the manager use?

主答 6 号，3/5 号接答；第 4、6、11 页。

**English:** For probability reliability, we examine log loss and complementary calibration and ranking measures. For a fixed contact capacity, we report buyers found. If the objective is merchandise value, the ranking rule changes. These answer different questions, so there is no single universal winner. The final operating choice needs cost and intervention evidence.

**中文：** 本稿把全体概率质量与商业选择并列，不能因路线选 B 就追认 D09 已独立正式确认。审阅后把实际采用口径如实写入记录。
