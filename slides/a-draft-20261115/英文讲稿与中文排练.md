## 第 1 页 · Finding repeat buyers with limited capacity
主讲：6 号｜35 秒｜约 72 词

### English
We are Six Sigma. We ask how a manager can identify repeat buyers when customer-contact capacity is limited. Using Olist's historical orders, we rank existing customers for repurchase within ninety days. We compare equally sized lists and use customer groups to understand missed buyers. Our evidence covers the team's data audit, feature analysis, logistic regression, segmentation and checks. The output supports a candidate list and a content-test proposal. Marketing impact remains untested.

We link order items to orders and customers, and create one row per customer at each prediction date. The input contains six calendar months of earlier eligible orders. The target is at least one approved order with positive merchandise value in the following ninety days. We audit timestamps so that later delivery events or review responses cannot enter earlier predictors. ## 第 3 页 · Customer history suggests feature ideas
主讲：2 号｜55 秒｜约 106 词

### English
Our existing H05 segmentation uses K-means with three groups. It standardizes recency, log-transformed frequency and spending using October data, then assigns January customers to the learned centers. The labels summarize the resulting groups: recent single-order, long-inactive single-order and multi-order. They are not manual recency cut-offs or identical descriptions of every person. The long-inactive group has ninety repurchasers among twelve thousand three hundred and thirty customers. This motivates checking fulfilment and review information, with attention to availability and missingness. Member two proposed pending deliveries and delivery time, and mean review score with review coverage. Those proposals differ from the variants already tested. Their predictive benefit remains untested.

### 中文排练

## 第 4 页 · Weighting found four more repeat buyers
主讲：3 号｜75 秒｜约 157 词

### English
Our baseline is logistic regression using eight historical fields. Most customers do not repurchase, so we also test class weighting, which gives the rare positive class more influence during fitting. Both models use the same October fitting customers and the same January evaluation customers. Each January list contains two thousand nine hundred and thirty-seven people. The unweighted model finds forty-six repeat buyers, and the weighted model finds fifty. This is four additional buyers in this particular historical comparison. The weighted list contains about one point seven percent repeat buyers and captures twenty-one point seven four percent of all two hundred and thirty observed repeat buyers. Most selected customers still do not repurchase. The two lists also contain different people. We use the weighted model's score to rank customers, rather than interpreting it as a calibrated purchase probability. This is useful development evidence, not proof that weighting will always win or that contacting these people creates extra sales.

### 中文排练
3 号的核心讲法：同一训练/评价客户、同名单容量，46→50净增4。加权是让稀少正例在拟合时有更大权重，不是只训练正例。precision=50/2937=1.70%；recall=50/230=21.74%。不称统计显著或稳定获胜。

## 第 5 页 · The tested additions did not beat the baseline
主讲：5 号｜75 秒｜约 150 词

### English
We then check whether the tested extensions improve the same January list. The weighted baseline finds fifty buyers. Adding the tested delivery fields finds forty-eight, review fields forty-six, a group label fifty, and separate group models forty-nine. The separate models use a global fallback for the small multi-order group. None beats the baseline at the ten-percent capacity, although some variants do better at other capacities. This conclusion applies only to the fields actually tested. It does not reject the latest feature combinations proposed by member two. We also retain the October five-fold diagnostic. The unweighted baseline averages seven hits per fold and the weighted baseline six point eight, so weighting does not win every check. These folds each select four hundred and twenty-four people and cannot be compared directly with the January count of fifty. Together, these checks support keeping a simple reference while reporting where improvements were not observed.

### 中文排练
5 号负责核数与反馈：只讲实际 F1/F2 和群模型。模型代码中的 B/C 是候选编号，不是展示路线A/B，画面改用完整名称避混。CV均值7 vs6.8表示内部诊断也有反例；不是五个月、不是一月50的置信区间。

## 第 6 页 · The global list still misses most long-inactive buyers
主讲：5 号｜55 秒｜约 129 词

### English
We connect the model scores to the same customer-group labels. In the current weighted global list, the recent single-order group receives two thousand one hundred and ninety places and contributes twenty-one hits. The long-inactive group receives seventy-three places and contributes two hits. The multi-order group receives six hundred and seventy-four places and contributes twenty-seven hits. Those add up to the same fifty buyers. For the long-inactive group, eighty-eight of its ninety observed buyers remain outside the list. We therefore run a separate diagnostic: rank only this group and select exactly seventy-nine people. The unweighted model finds one buyer and the weighted model two. This small difference is descriptive. Increasing a group's share of the global list is not the same as improving its ranking at a fixed group capacity.

## 第 7 页 · Customer profiles support a testable content idea
主讲：4 号｜65 秒｜约 150 词

### English
The customer groups also support a business hypothesis. The long-inactive group has a median of one historical order and a median recency of about one hundred and thirty-five days. Historical category coverage suggests a home-goods buying guide as a candidate theme. This does not establish a distinctive preference or a causal effect. The existing proposal randomly allocates reachable, consenting customers equally between a themed guide and a generic shopping guide. The channel, format and timing stay the same. The main outcome is repurchase within ninety days, using all randomly allocated customers in the denominator. The trial has not been implemented. Sample size is a constraint: detecting a fifty-percent relative increase requires about twenty-one thousand three hundred and eight people, more than the historical group size. We need to check reachability and statistical power before launch. Both groups receive contact, so this design tests the theme, not contact versus no contact.

### 中文排练
4 号延续自己的分群、品类覆盖和试验设计。保留两组均触达、1:1、ITT分母、未开展和样本量不足。主题按历史覆盖选，不说久单独有偏好或已证有效。+50%是相对提升，不是+50个百分点。

## 第 9 页 · Eight inputs and one ranking protocol
主讲：1 / 3 号｜备查

### English
The main reference is current R3 F0 weighted logistic regression, fitted on all October customers. The eight fields are recency, order frequency, merchandise spending, average item rows, freight share, recent thirty-day orders, recent thirty-day spending, and state. Numeric preprocessing uses log-one-plus, median imputation with missing indicators, and standard scaling. State uses most-frequent imputation and one-hot encoding. The recorded model specifies C equal to one, balanced class weights, a maximum of two thousand iterations and random state 2026. The final list sorts scores in descending order, with customer ID as a deterministic tie-break. We do not describe this raw score as a natural probability or the observed development score as a blind-test estimate.

### 中文排练
八字段是客户摘要，不是原始九表只有八列。模型配置来自当前R3源码，当前采用是展示工作基线，不伪造事前冻结；历史包的完整环境/终止诊断缺口仍按原记录保留。

## 第 10 页 · More group places need not mean better group ranking
主讲：4 / 5 号｜备查

### English
The global and group-only comparisons have different denominators. The baseline gives the long-inactive group 73 global places and finds two buyers. Adding group ID gives it 181 places and still finds two. The separate group models give it 518 places and find four. But when each method gets exactly 79 places within that group, the results are two, two and one. Therefore the four global hits from group models do not prove better ranking within the group. Global capacity is fixed, but allocation across groups changes.

### 中文排练
用此表回答群模型为什么没采用：518席4人是覆盖变化；固定79仅1人。禁止用“全局同名额”推导“群内同名额”。

## 第 11 页 · Capacity and internal validation show trade-offs
主讲：3 / 5 / 6 号｜备查

### English
At five percent, the unweighted and weighted baseline both find 31 buyers. At ten percent, they find 46 and 50. At twenty percent, they find 73 and 75. Other candidates can perform better at different capacities, so ten percent is a comparison choice rather than a proven optimum. The October five-fold means are seven and six point eight hits for unweighted and weighted logistic regression. The corresponding sample standard deviations are about two point five five and one point nine two. They describe variability across the five folds and are not confidence intervals. Group-method CV additionally conditions on a clustering representation learned from all October unlabeled features, so it is not a fully fold-contained clustering-and-classification pipeline.

### 中文排练
capacity变化有取舍：5%31/31，10%46/50，20%73/75。CV每折N4232/K424；std是样本SD，不是95%CI、不是真正未来独立测试。
